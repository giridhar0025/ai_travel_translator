Travel Translator AI ("the app", "we") is provided by Divami. This policy
explains what data the app processes and why.

## Data we process

- **Account data (only if you sign in):** if you sign in with email or Google,
  we receive your email address, display name, and profile photo to identify
  your account. **Guest mode collects no personal identifiers** — an anonymous
  account id is generated on our server.
- **Content you translate:** the text you enter and the voice audio you record
  are sent to our backend and to our translation/speech provider to produce
  translations and transcriptions.
- **Activity:** translations you make are stored as conversation history and
  recent items, associated with your (anonymous or signed-in) account, so you
  can revisit them.
- **Microphone:** used only while you are actively recording a voice input. Audio
  is transmitted for transcription and is not used for any other purpose.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the app
contains **no ads**.

## How data is used
Solely to provide the app's functionality: translation, speech-to-text,
text-to-speech playback, and your history.

## Third parties
- **Hugging Face Inference API** — processes your text and audio to generate
  translations, transcriptions, and synthesized speech.
- **Firebase Authentication (Google)** — used only if you choose to sign in.
- **Railway** — hosts our backend and database.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

## Data transfer & security
All network traffic between the app and our backend uses encrypted transport
(HTTPS/TLS).

## Data retention & deletion
You can delete your account and associated data at any time from the app
(Settings), which removes your profile and conversation history from our servers.
For assistance, contact us at the address below.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will reflect any changes.
