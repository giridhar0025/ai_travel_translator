## Full description (≤4000)
Travel Translator AI helps you communicate anywhere. Type or speak in your
language and get instant, natural translations you can play aloud to the person
in front of you.

Features
• Real-time text translation across English, Hindi, Telugu, and Thai
• Voice input — speak and get an accurate transcription to translate
• Play translations aloud with natural pronunciation, plus a slow-speech mode
• Conversation history so you can revisit past translations
• Quick Phrases for restaurants, directions, transport, shopping, and emergencies
• Context modes (Formal, Casual, Tourist) for tone-appropriate wording
• Works instantly in guest mode — no sign-up required to start

Whether you're ordering food, asking for directions, or handling an emergency
abroad, Travel Translator AI bridges the language gap in seconds.
